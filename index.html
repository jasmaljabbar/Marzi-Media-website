FAQ

Questions businesses ask us

Where is Marzi Media based?

Marzi Media is based at AI Zone, Ambalavayal, in Wayanad district, Kerala. Businesses anywhere in Wayanad, including Kalpetta, Sulthan Bathery and Mananthavady, can reach us on WhatsApp or by phone.

What services does Marzi Media offer?

Ten services from one team: branding and creative design, digital marketing, video production and animation, web and software development, AI and business automation, IT infrastructure and networking, business and digital solutions, interior design, CCTV and security, and printing and signage.

How do I get a quote?

Message us on WhatsApp or use the contact form. Tell us what you need and we will share a clear plan, scope and cost before any work starts.

Can you handle our branding, website and marketing together?

Yes. One team plans your brand, website and campaigns together, so everything stays consistent and you deal with one partner instead of several vendors.

Do you install CCTV and office networks as well?

Yes. Alongside design and marketing, our technology team plans and installs CCTV systems, office networks and structured cabling.

Do you create content in Malayalam?

Yes. We create posters, ads and social media content in English and Malayalam, so your message reaches local customers in the language they use. See examples.
